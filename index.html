Ductwork vs. Structural Steel Conflict

During a pilot project, ConstructAid.AI flagged a critical conflict between ductwork routing and structural steel beam placement that a senior Project Manager had missed during initial review. The AI caught discrepancies with article 7.4.2.3 on structural steel spec, noting beam supports that did not align with the approved ductwork layout.

By catching this in pre-construction, the team avoided what would have been a $50,000+ rework cost. It's exactly this type of detail-oriented analysis across thousands of spec lines that makes AI-powered oversight invaluable — no matter how experienced your team is.
